Native apps & custom software

Development

Our current range includes apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac. We also offer Linux and FreeBSD command-line development and web server solutions on request.

Our Apple apps are built natively with SwiftUI and Metal, without third-party frameworks or dependencies. We do not use cross-platform JavaScript technologies or similar solutions.

Our apps do not contain analytics or tracking modules and do not collect data in any way. We believe in clean, transparent software.
