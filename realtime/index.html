Design & maintenance

Realtime solutions

Real-time 3D rendering and other responsive techniques let users adapt an experience on the spot as needs evolve. Changes can be made without reprogramming the entire system, re-editing footage or rendering it again.

Visual quality

Close to the final image

Real-time computer graphics cannot always match the detail and finish of imagery produced by a render farm over thousands of hours. With the right techniques, though, the final result can come remarkably close.

Fast iteration

Ideas, brought to life

This approach is hard to beat when you need to assemble a compelling visualisation quickly from existing modules and a simple floor plan, or bring a creator’s spontaneous idea to life during theatre rehearsals.

Our experience

Many formats, one creative toolkit

Broadcast & interactive

Virtual studios

Real-time spaces and backgrounds for broadcast and interactive experiences. These environments can be dynamic, modular and easy to reconfigure, built in Unreal Engine, Unity, Aximmetry or a custom solution powered by a proprietary 3D engine.

We can create a complex show and camera control system tailored to your needs, with a simple custom control panel for operators. They can run the production without needing to understand the entire system in depth. More advanced users can even change a scene while it is running, without restarting it or waiting through a lengthy rebuild.

Art & events

Visuals & Projection

Once a building projection site or event venue has been selected, a 3D model can be created quickly. This lets us check how the projected content will fit the target surfaces and appear from the intended viewing distance before expensive equipment is deployed. The technique makes the director’s or artist’s work much easier, allowing them to see their ideas take shape in a form close to the final result.

PREVISUAL

Animated visualisations and concepts

Before committing to an expensive offline render, it can be useful to assemble a quick, low-cost scene to review ideas, camera movements and lighting angles with the performers. This can reduce the workload and avoid problems in post-production. With camera tracking and either background projection or an LED wall, footage can even be captured immediately, without a green screen or unreliable chroma keying.
