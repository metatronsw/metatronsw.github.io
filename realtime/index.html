Broadcast & interactive

Virtual studios

Real-time spaces and backgrounds for broadcast and interactive experiences. These environments can be dynamic, modular and easy to reconfigure, built in Unreal Engine, Unity, Aximmetry or a custom solution powered by a proprietary 3D engine.

We can create a complex show and camera control system tailored to your needs, with a simple custom control panel for operators. They can run the production without understanding the entire system in depth. More advanced users can even change a scene while it is running, without restarting it or waiting through a lengthy rebuild.

Interactive experiences

Interactive installations

The first Regional Digital Summit took place on 17–18 November 2016 at Millenáris Park in Budapest.

The event explored the challenges of the digital economy and society across the V4 countries, Austria and Germany, with a focus on autonomous vehicles, Industry 4.0, blockchain technology and fintech, and startup ecosystems.

Magyar Telekom and Ericsson Hungary demonstrated download speeds of 1,200 Mbps using 4G+ technology.

For this special occasion, T-Systems commissioned an interactive information desk with a three-screen panorama and an additional touchscreen. The presentation ran automatically, while visitors could use the controls at any time to switch topics or look freely around the virtual environment as the camera moved through it.

Touch-screen panorama installation

The installation introduced the then-emerging concept of the Internet of Things (IoT) and its potential applications through a detailed, visually engaging presentation. It was later exhibited at numerous locations across Hungary, where visitors could explore and try it for themselves.

Art & events

Visuals & Projection

Once a building projection site or event venue has been selected, a 3D model can be created quickly. This lets us check how the projected content will fit the target surfaces and appear from the intended viewing distance before expensive equipment is deployed. The technique makes the director’s or artist’s work much easier, allowing them to see their ideas take shape in a form close to the final result.

O.Z.O.R.A. Festival - Chill Dome

For complex, large-budget productions, we provided more than carefully composed still images: clients could explore the envisioned space in real time, complete with the proposed design elements. This allowed production teams, artists and clients to share suggestions and feedback together on the spot, with changes often implemented immediately, taking the presentation to a new level.

PREVISUAL

Animated visualisations and concepts

Before committing to an expensive offline render, it can be useful to assemble a quick, low-cost scene to review ideas, camera movements and lighting angles with the performers. This can reduce the workload and avoid problems in post-production. With camera tracking and either background projection or an LED wall, footage can even be captured immediately, without a green screen or unreliable chroma keying.

TRIVIADOR

Filming a live-action game show

This pilot explored a live-action adaptation of Honfoglaló, a game popular in Hungary and around the world.

Contestants followed the action on a green game map on the central table. In the camera feed, this surface was replaced with a detailed 3D landscape, with towers and other elements rising above the tabletop. Green-screen compositing combined the physical set with the virtual environment, while a real-time preview allowed the camera operator to frame each shot with the digital elements in view.

Unfortunately, the technical limitations of that time, particularly the early camera-tracking systems, prevented the project from progressing beyond the pilot.
