A broad perspective

From the first brief to the final handover.

My early work focused on interactive media, websites and a storage and inventory system. I later worked as a freelance photographer, graphic designer and project manager, creating print publications, interactive installations and catalogue systems, alongside light painting and event projects.

I have experience throughout the full project lifecycle: understanding requirements, design, implementation, automation, testing, delivery and training. Working with clients and other contractors has been a central part of both my employed and independent work.

Virtual studio development strengthened my focus on UI and UX. After further work with television companies on studio construction, programme-control tools and content creation, I moved towards the Apple ecosystem; since 2020, I have been developing my own apps as an independent developer.
